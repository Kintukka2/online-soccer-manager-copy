# online-soccer-manager-copy

A study and future re-implementation of **Online Soccer Manager (OSM)**, the free-to-play multiplayer football management game by Gamebasics / Miniclip Nederland.

## Documentation

- [`docs/OSM-ANALYSIS.md`](docs/OSM-ANALYSIS.md) is the complete analysis of OSM. It covers:
  - the history and business
  - the core loop and season structure
  - competitions
  - player model and match engine
  - tactics and the community meta
  - training, transfers, finances, staff and boosts
  - monetisation, live ops, social, fair play and reception
  - a domain-model blueprint for building a copy
- [`docs/COMPANION-LANDSCAPE.md`](docs/COMPANION-LANDSCAPE.md) maps the companion apps, sites, bots and creators around OSM. It also covers the Terms of Service constraints, a feature gap matrix and positioning for a fut.gg/futbin-style companion.
- [`docs/brand/`](docs/brand/) is the OSMWiz brand and visual system. It contains:
  - [`OSMWiz_Visual_System.html`](docs/brand/OSMWiz_Visual_System.html), the live visual system;
  - [`BRAND-GUIDE.md`](docs/brand/BRAND-GUIDE.md), the written brand and style guide;
  - [`tokens.css`](docs/brand/tokens.css) and [`tokens.json`](docs/brand/tokens.json), the design tokens;
  - [`EVIDENCE.md`](docs/brand/EVIDENCE.md), the sources, method and every measured value.
